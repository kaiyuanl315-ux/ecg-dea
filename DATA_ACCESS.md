# Data access and distributed material

The supplement distributes scientific source files and selected group-level summaries. It does not distribute raw clinical records, patient identifiers, encounter mappings, individual predictions, ECG input arrays, fitted comparator serialization files, private manifests, individual QRS decisions, or manuscript and submission documents.

The supplied manuscript identifies MIMIC-IV v3.0, MIMIC-IV-ED v2.2 and MIMIC-IV-ECG v1.0 as study resources. Its access statement directs users to the original PhysioNet resources and their current credentialing, training, licensing and data-use terms. Linking ECGs to restricted clinical records requires the applicable clinical-data permissions. The historical README's MIMIC-IV v2.2 citation belongs to the original release and should not be read as the version used by the current additional analyses.

Zhongshan Hospital source data are restricted by privacy and institutional requirements. Their inclusion in the study does not authorize redistribution in this repository. Requests and institutional review follow the manuscript's data-access statement. No additional right to distribute source records or third-party resources is created by this code supplement.

Aggregate tables retain their study definitions. They are not synthetic patient datasets and cannot substitute for individual inputs when refitting a model or reproducing bootstrap uncertainty. The IG files summarize groups of encounters. Figure S8A's individual-encounter lead shares and their linkage order are excluded even though the display labels are anonymous.
